# 120 film and edge-reference audit, 2026-11-06

Scope: all 11 previously unsupported camera-film profiles, plus the five new SpektraLab camera films. The nine previously reviewed physical 120 stocks and two Portra push aliases are unchanged. Photographic paper is not camera roll film; `ilford_multigrade_iv_rc` and the legacy 2302 print-material artwork are excluded.

Two independent questions must be answered: did this particular product exist in 120, and is there sufficient evidence to author its particular edges? Confirming a box or an emulsion does not establish edge typography, two-sided phase, printing era, or long-format coverage. Missing artwork does not mean a film never existed in 120.

Evidence levels: **A** is a manufacturer document/product page, including a clearly identified archived manufacturer document. **B** is an inspected actual-film photograph or scan with identified provenance. **C** is a specialist/retailer record. The initial runtime additions are explicitly approximate drawings based on A+B, not real scan assets or exact factory replicas. The later user-approved CineStill v8 reference is separately labeled U (user-supplied visual evidence with unverified provenance). No third-party image pixels are included.

## Remaining source requirements

For still-film products whose 120 existence is established, the useful next source is a readable, identified, continuous uncut strip showing both actual film edges over at least the nominal 174 mm crop, preferably with a ruler or known camera gate. A manufacturer full-period drawing may also suffice after explicit review of its inference limits. A box, backing paper, one 135 strip, a cropped single frame, an AI border, an existing app's generated border, or a generic emulsion-family alias does not meet that requirement.

For converted cinema stocks, additionally identify the converter, slit position, stock width, perforation geometry and retail product era. The existing unperforated 61 mm model must not silently misrepresent a perforated conversion. No such unverified layout is enabled here.

## Validation scope

The v7 reproducible generator checks exact hashes, opaque neutral RGB, finite bounds, explicit mark inventory and no extrapolation. The v9 generator separately distinguishes observed marks from its declared periodic extension and checks an original-position metadata gap. Android tests cover snapshot identity, finite crop rejection, neutral B&W negative display, unchanged color-negative behavior and exact preview/float-compositor parity for every supported format, orientation and route. These are software checks, not historical authenticity certification or final JPEG/TIFF codec validation. See `120_EXPANSION_VALIDATION_20261106.md` for executed results.

The upstream scientific profiles retain their original 135/fixed-development measurements where so labeled. This audit and implementation concern the physical border presentation, not an invented 120-specific emulsion or base-density calibration.
