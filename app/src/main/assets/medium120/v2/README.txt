These seven assets are new authored illustrations guided by independently inspected real-film photographs or manufacturer diagrams. They are not photographic cutouts or factory-calibrated reproductions. Typeface, ink RGB, pitch and phase remain approximate. No third-party photograph pixels, fake production codes, perforations or light leaks are distributed.

The earlier nine PNGs and their v1 hashes remain unchanged for frozen export jobs. New snapshots select the latest stock revision.
