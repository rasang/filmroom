## Five reviewed still-film pairs, approved 2026-11-06

The selector now includes original 135 + bare 120 pairs for Kodak Tri-X 400TX,
Kodak T-MAX 100, Fujifilm NEOPAN ACROS II, Ilford HP5 PLUS and CineStill 800T.
They retain the previously approved procedural geometry, UVs, physical scale,
camera and lighting. The ten labels are original simplified typesetting based
on inspected visible product faces, not scans or traced proprietary logotypes.
No third-party photograph, model, font software or review-board image is shipped.
Labels are typeset with DejaVu Sans; no font binaries are bundled. DejaVu's
font software is based on the Bitstream Vera license, with DejaVu changes in the
public domain; it is not SIL OFL. This does not apply a new license to the renders.
Brand names and packaging marks remain their owners' rights, without endorsement.
Existing GPL project licensing and scientific profile-data attribution remain
separate; the profile data's CC BY-SA license is not assigned to these original
packaging illustrations.

The five accepted thumbnails are copied without resizing or re-encoding.
`manifest.json` pins their exact bytes, current complete selector coverage and
product-page references. The separate editable source deliverable is
`Filmroom-five-roll-models-review.zip`, SHA-256
`19bfc4c70d4177f67f869eb2894ece877fb49c85d1933b00586850f2f49621c4`.
It retains original labels, Blender models, provenance, and geometry/image QA.
The source pack's review-only status records its state before user approval;
these five thumbnails were subsequently approved for application integration.

The illustrations show physical packaging forms, not available output borders.
No approved 135 strip exists for these five profiles. Only 400TX, T-MAX 100 and
HP5 PLUS currently have approved 120 strips; ACROS II and 800T do not. Roll art
must never enable a frame format or borrow another film's strip. 400TX is not
7266, and 800T does not borrow a Vision3 5219 motion-picture can.

Packaging is a reference-informed simplification of the observed versions, not
a manufacturer-certified reconstruction. The 120 top disc and narrow slot retain
the shared model's simplification. The inspected CineStill 120 face shows a red
CineStill seal; no unobserved 800T lettering is added to that face. Product-page
references in the manifest document appearance only and grant no right to
redistribute the original photographs.
