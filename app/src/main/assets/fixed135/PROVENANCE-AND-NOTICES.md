# Film border artwork — provenance and notices

These are reference-informed generated textures with programmatic film geometry and edge-code correction, not redistributed original film scans. The application uses a fixed 36×24 mm photo opening, user-customized 25 markings, and a chosen crop phase. Colors, font shapes and film texture are reconstructions, not claims of pixel-identical scans.

Kodak, Fujifilm, film names and other marks identify depicted stocks; no affiliation or endorsement is claimed. Linked photographs and technical documents remain subject to their respective rights; none is included in this asset package. No blanket license to third-party references is asserted.

## User-selectable negative hole presentation (introduced v10)

The “Negative white holes” preference defaults ON: all18 physical stocks /20 profiles show pure white #FFFFFF perforation holes. OFF changes only negative stocks to black #000000; the four reversal stocks stay white. The preference remains stored while the watermark is off and is then disabled in the UI. This is an explicit aesthetic choice, not new evidence of original scan appearance. Rail colors, lettering, geometry, feather and crop are unchanged. All prior28 contracts remain immutable for queued jobs. Before the v11 typography revision below, ten white revisions plus four black alternatives brought the registry to42 contracts using22 WebP payloads.

## Historical v9 reference-specific presentation contract

The current package contains 18 physical frames mapped to 20 profiles. Portra 800, push +1 and push +2 share one physical frame; the printed “800-3” is a stock generation, not a push setting. User-customized 25/25A fields are distinct from factory production identifiers. Motion-picture frames do not use consumer DX/25A or fabricated KEYKODE batch data.

Four frames (Ektar100, Gold200, Ultramax400, Portra800) use individually supported positive-display color reconstructions. Six additional frames change only their final compositor hole matte. These six PNGs are unchanged. All 18 PNGs remain byte-identical to v6; current and historical matte versions must remain distinct for queued jobs.

**Limits:** No exact manufacturing RGB, original-scanner calibration, original-font identity or pixel-identical scan claim is made. Nine negative-stock DX payloads have independently verified stock/generation and customized frame bits. Correct logical bits do not prove factory physical phase or dimensions. New final compositor/codec validation is an integration requirement, not established by reference photographs.

### Hole presentation summary

- Dark holes with own-stock reference support: Portra160/400/800, Gold200, Ultramax400, Ektar100, C200, XTRA400, Vision3 250D and 200T.
- Light holes with viewed positive/transparency reference support: E100, Kodachrome64, Provia100F and Velvia100.
- Still provisional, without newly verified positive full-hole evidence: PRO400H, Vision3 50D, Vision3 500T and Verita200D. Do not describe these colors as authenticated original scans.

Physical film photographed against a light or dark backing and different scan workflows can produce different hole appearances. The historical v9 per-stock matte followed the named reference, not a universal rule. The current v10 selectable matte instead follows the explicit aesthetic choice above.
