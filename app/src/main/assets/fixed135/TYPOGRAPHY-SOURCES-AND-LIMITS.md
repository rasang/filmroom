# Film-border reference drawing: typography revision v11

## Scope and authenticity limits
The 2026-10-31 audit personally checked own-stock physical film or actual scans for 15 of 18 physical stocks. This is partial evidence coverage, NOT 15 complete replicas or 18 authenticated raw scans. Actual-image gaps remain for Fujifilm PRO400H, Kodak VISION3 50D, and Kodak Verita200D; their current assets remain official-document reference drawings with these limits. Individual photographs vary in film generation, process, lighting, resolution and scan correction. No universal RGB or manufacturing-tolerance claim follows.

18 physical stocks map to20 profiles; Portra800,+1,+2 share one physical frame. Frame25 is user customization. User-selectable white/dark hole backgrounds are presentation choices, not claims that every physical scan has those hole colors.

## Portra800
Primary physical strip: Analog.Cafe, https://www.analog.cafe/r/the-biggest-film-test-and-a-new-film-inversion-app-h4w9 . Original public image: https://res.cloudinary.com/analog-cafe/image/upload/image-froth_680572_-dDb4iFhZ.avif .
Readably KODAK PORTRA 800-3. Restored word gap before800 and widened compressed generated letter groups toward observed relative hole-pitch proportions. The suffix -3 is film generation, not push. Bowed physical negative and perspective allow approximate visual proportions, not exact font metrics. Existing own generated ink/texture was reused; no reference image pixels were copied. Existing positive-presentation colors and all DX bits remain unchanged.
